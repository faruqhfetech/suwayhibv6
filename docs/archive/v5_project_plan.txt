SUWAYHIB v5 -- PROJECT PLAN
A closed-vocabulary streaming verifier, distilled from the v4 teacher.

Written before building. Compare against v4_planning_superseded.txt, which
was also written before building and turned out to be wrong about its
central choice. Expect this document to be revised by what happens.


===============================================================================
1. THE RESEARCH QUESTION
===============================================================================

    Can a small closed-vocabulary verifier match forced-decoding accuracy
    at a fraction of the compute?

v4 is the baseline. The answer may be NO -- that is a finding, and a more
useful one than most positive results, because it would establish a floor on
how much model this task actually needs.

Success is NOT "smaller and faster". A 15M student beating a 72.6M teacher on
speed is arithmetic, not a result. Success is MATCHING IT ON ACCURACY --
specifically on the segmental discrimination (s vs s, h vs h) that v3 could
not do at all and v4 does at 0.994 vs 0.000.


===============================================================================
2. WHY THE TASK IS SMALLER THAN v4 TREATS IT
===============================================================================

v4 runs whisper-base-quran: 72.6M parameters, a general ASR model able to
produce any of ~50,000 tokens in any order.

The actual task:
  - the verse is KNOWN before the learner recites
  - Juz Amma contains ~2,200 distinct word types
  - the question is never "what did they say" but "did they say THIS"

That is verification, not recognition. The model never has to search a
vocabulary; it has to check a hypothesis. Much of v4's capacity is paying
for generality the task does not use.


===============================================================================
3. THE MODEL
===============================================================================

    input   an audio chunk  +  the expected word (text)
    output  p(the expected word completes at this frame)

    audio encoder    causal / limited-lookahead, ~8M, runs per chunk
    text encoder     ~2M, characters WITH harakat, PRECOMPUTED offline
    cross-attention  ~2M, the text queries the audio frames
    completion head  per-frame probability

    ~12-15M parameters total.

DESIGN DECISIONS AND THEIR EVIDENCE

  CROSS-ATTENTION, NOT TWO-TOWER.
    Late fusion pools audio to a single vector and destroys the phoneme
    detail that separates s from s. That is v4a, which scored holdout AP
    0.064. Cross-attention is the mechanism that let v4 localise an error to
    a single kasra. Use the thing with evidence behind it.

  TEXT AS CHARACTERS WITH DIACRITICS, NOT PHONEMES.
    Arabic is nearly phonemic once vowelled, and v4 proved the model uses
    harakat: every diacritic-stripped text variant scored EXACTLY 0.000.
    Characters avoid building a phoneme lexicon and tajweed rule set.

  TEXT EMBEDDINGS PRECOMPUTED.
    Closed vocabulary. Embed all ~2,200 word types once at build time and
    ship them. At inference only the audio encoder and cross-attention run.
    This is the speed advantage a general model structurally cannot have.

  OUTPUT IS A PROBABILITY, NOT A BINARY.
    One head gives three things: did it appear (any frame above threshold),
    where (which frame), and how confidently (the peak). The third is what
    allows "found, but weak" instead of forcing a verdict -- the same
    property that made v4's single global threshold workable.

  CAUSAL ENCODER.
    No lookahead, so it runs on each incoming chunk. This is what makes
    streaming possible and is the most likely source of accuracy loss
    against offline v4.


===============================================================================
4. TRAINING
===============================================================================

STAGE 1 -- DISTILLATION

    for each (reciter, ayah):
        generate N augmented / corrupted waveform variants
        run the v4 teacher, record per-word worst-token probability
        train the student to reproduce that number

  The teacher's speaker invariance came from thousands of Tarteel voices.
  Distillation transfers it into a small student WITHOUT needing those
  voices. That is the answer to the six-speaker limit that killed v4a: do
  not learn invariance from six speakers, inherit it from a model that
  learned it from thousands.

  AUGMENTATION MUST HAPPEN AT THE WAVEFORM, BEFORE THE TEACHER.
  The teacher has to SEE the augmented audio so its targets reflect it.
  v4a cached features and thereby made waveform augmentation impossible --
  do not repeat that.

  CORRUPTION IS NOT OPTIONAL. Clean audio gives the teacher ~1.000 almost
  everywhere (measured: Husary median 1.0000 over 160 words). A student
  trained on that learns to output 1.0 always and never flags anything.
  Deliberate errors are what create a RANGE of targets and therefore a
  gradient.

  HARD NEGATIVES ARE WHERE THIS LIVES OR DIES. Random wrong words are
  rejected trivially and stop teaching within an epoch. The mix must lean on:
      - other words from the SAME ayah
      - duration-matched donors from the same reciter
      - minimal pairs: s/s, h/h, q/k, d/d
  pairs.py already implements this mining and carries over directly.

  OPEN: the corrupted fraction. Too low and the student says yes to
  everything; too high and it says no. Start with a pilot, look at the
  target distribution, then set it. Do not guess it up front.

STAGE 2 -- TASK FINE-TUNING

  Train on the actual decision -- correct vs incorrect -- using real
  labelled data:
      - matched learner pairs (v4 has Recording_5 correct / Recording_6
        deliberate s->s on the same verse)
      - QDAT: 1500+ voices, correct and incorrect recitation of three
        tajweed rules, public

  THIS IS WHERE THE STUDENT CAN EXCEED THE TEACHER. On raw acoustic
  modelling it cannot -- a distilled student is bounded by its teacher. But
  the teacher was optimised for TRANSCRIPTION, not for discriminating
  correct from incorrect. Fine-tuning on the real decision is a different
  objective, and the student can be better at it.


===============================================================================
5. THE STREAMING PIPELINE
===============================================================================

The model answers one question. A programmed state machine turns that into a
practice session.

    maintain a cursor at the next expected word
    endpoint / trim silence and noise on the incoming stream
    ask the verifier for the word at the cursor
      found, confident  -> CONFIRM it, advance the cursor
      found, weak       -> hold; keep listening
      not found, audio continuing -> hold; they may still be saying it
      not found, timeout or silence -> PAUSE and ask them to take it again
                                       from this word

  STRICTLY SEQUENTIAL, NO LOOKAHEAD. Wait for word 3 before considering word
  4. This costs the ability to detect a later error while an earlier one is
  unresolved, but it removes cascade failure entirely: the system can never
  drift out of alignment because it never advances past something
  unresolved.

  ON TIMEOUT, ASK -- DO NOT DECIDE. The system does not have to work out WHY
  a word is missing (skipped, mispronounced, still coming, stopped). It only
  has to notice it is stuck, and then ask the learner to repeat from that
  word. That is what a teacher does, and it collapses four hard
  classification problems into one easy one.

  THIS LOWERS THE ACCURACY BAR. "I did not hear that, let us take it again"
  costs one repetition when wrong. "You got that wrong" costs trust. Same
  insight as v3's replay-is-the-message, applied to a streaming loop.

  RESUME FROM A KNOWN WORD, not from wherever the learner is. Keeps the
  sequential invariant intact, and re-reciting a phrase IS the practice.


===============================================================================
6. THE INTERFACE
===============================================================================

Different from v3/v4, and it is the more interesting half of this version.

  Feedback becomes a STATE, not an EVENT. The verse is displayed; words
  confirm as they are recited. The learner watches progress instead of
  waiting for a verdict.

  CONFIRMATION IS MONOTONIC AND NEVER RETRACTED. A word that lights up and
  then un-lights is worse than no feedback at all. This implies a
  CONSERVATIVE confirmation threshold: leaving a correct word unconfirmed
  for another moment is cheap; confirming wrongly is not.

  THE VISIBLE STATE IS "CONFIRMED SO FAR", NOT "ERRORS". The device never
  says anything is wrong -- it just stops confirming. The learner notices
  the words stopped lighting up. Information without accusation, which is
  the same principle as the replay, expressed continuously.

  RISK: a fluent learner outruns the display. If they recite three words in
  the time the system confirms one, the highlight lags and feels broken.
  Open question whether confirmation must beat real-time or whether lag is
  tolerable.


===============================================================================
7. EVALUATION
===============================================================================

  REUSE testset/ EXACTLY. Do not regenerate. It is what makes v3, v4 and v5
  comparable, and regenerating it destroys the whole comparison.

  REPORT THE CONTROL-SEAM FALSE-POSITIVE GAP FIRST, as in v3 and v4. If the
  student is reading splice artifacts, nothing else means anything.

  REPORT PER TIER, never a single aggregate F1. An aggregate over a mixture
  we chose is a statistic about our sampling.

  THE COMPARISON TABLE:
                            v3        v4        v5
      detection AUPRC       0.901     -         ?
      top-1 localization    0.543     -         ?
      s vs s separation     ~none     0.994/0.000  ?
      params                0         72.6M     ~15M
      CPU per attempt       ~1s       0.48s     ?

  ACCURACY-MATCHED OR IT IS NOT A RESULT.

  CHECK THAT A RANDOM MODEL FAILS THE METRIC. In v4 an UNTRAINED head scored
  0.993 on a device-localization metric we had built, because the metric was
  asking an easy question. Run this check before trusting any number.

  THE UNCONTAMINATED TEST is a real voice not in Tarteel's training data.
  v4's seven learner recordings are the only such data in the project.


===============================================================================
8. RISKS, HONESTLY
===============================================================================

  DISTILLED STUDENTS ARE BOUNDED BY THEIR TEACHERS. Stage 1 cannot exceed
  v4. Only stage 2, on a different objective, can. If QDAT and learner data
  do not materialise, the ceiling is v4 and the RQ becomes "how close, how
  cheap" rather than "can it beat it".

  STREAMING WILL COST ACCURACY. Causal encoding sees less context than
  offline forced decoding. Budget for the offline student to work first and
  the streaming student to be worse.

  SIX SPEAKERS IS STILL SIX SPEAKERS. Distillation is the proposed answer,
  but it is a HYPOTHESIS. If the student inherits the teacher's outputs
  without inheriting its invariance, this is v4a again with a different
  head. TEST THIS EARLY: evaluate on held-out reciters after stage 1, before
  building any of the streaming machinery.

  THE PIPELINE MAY MATTER MORE THAN THE MODEL. Endpointing, timeouts, and
  the confirm/hold/pause logic may dominate the experience regardless of how
  good the verifier is. That is arguably GOOD for an AI systems design
  course, but it means model metrics will not tell the whole story.


===============================================================================
9. ORDER OF WORK
===============================================================================

  1. Teacher extraction: augment + corrupt, run v4, dump targets.
     PILOT FIRST -- a few hundred examples, then look at the target
     distribution before scaling. The corrupted fraction should be set by
     what the teacher actually produces, not guessed.

  2. Student, OFFLINE first. Non-causal encoder, whole-window input. Prove
     the verification task is learnable at 15M before adding streaming.

  3. Held-out-reciter evaluation. THE GO / NO-GO GATE. If the student does
     not transfer across voices here, distillation did not solve the
     six-speaker problem and the approach is dead. Better to know at this
     point than after building the pipeline.

  4. Compare against v4 on testset/, same harness, same tiers.

  5. Only then: causal encoder, streaming pipeline, new interface.

  6. Stage 2 fine-tuning, if labelled data exists.

  KILL CRITERIA. If step 3 shows the same collapse v4a showed -- strong on
  seen voices, weak on unseen -- stop. Write it up as a second, stronger
  negative result: distillation from a well-generalising teacher was NOT
  sufficient to transfer speaker invariance into a small student under a
  six-speaker data regime. That is a genuinely useful finding, and it is
  better than three more weeks of tuning.

10. WHAT CARRIES OVER FROM v4
===============================================================================

  textscore.py    THE TEACHER. Imported, not just referenced.
  reftext.py      text preparation. The three rules (simple-plain, strip
                  U+0670, strip basmala) are already validated at 0.9994.
  injector.py     corruption tooling, for generating low targets
  pairs.py        hard-negative mining
  testset/        the comparability anchor -- DO NOT REGENERATE
  manifest_full.csv, reference_library/, texts/

  NOT carried over: device.py, static/, baseline.py, and the whole embedding
  chain. A device comes after the student works, and it will need a
  different interface anyway.
