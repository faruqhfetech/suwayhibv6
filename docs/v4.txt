SUWAYHIB v4 -- WHAT WAS BUILT AND WHY
Revised at the end of the v4 build. Supersedes v4_planning_superseded.txt,
which proposed contrastive acoustic word embeddings. That approach was
implemented, measured, and abandoned. Section 3 records why -- the negative
result is the more useful half of this document.


===============================================================================
0. THE SHORT VERSION
===============================================================================

The device forced-decodes the learner's recitation against the KNOWN verse
text using a Whisper model fine-tuned on Qur'anic Arabic, and reads the
probability the model assigns to each token. Correct recitation scores near
1.0; a mispronounced word collapses toward 0.

  no training            the model was already trained for this
  no gallery             no reference embeddings, centroids or spreads
  no per-word threshold  one global value, 0.10, with two orders of
                         magnitude of margin either side
  no alignment step      the decoder handles it

This is Goodness of Pronunciation (Witt & Young, 2000) with a modern
backbone. The method is well known. What is ours: the corpus verification,
the artifact-controlled evaluation, the comparison across four approaches,
the orthography findings in section 5, and the device itself.


===============================================================================
1. MEASURED RESULTS
===============================================================================

WORD SCORES  (whisper-base-quran, prepared simple-plain text, worst token
per word)

  Husary, 160 words / 40 ayahs   median 1.0000  p05 0.9993  min 0.9913
                                 0.00% below the 0.10 threshold
  learner, correct, careful      0.51 - 1.00   (10 verses, no false flags)
  learner, deliberate s -> s     0.000
  wrong verse entirely           0.000  (every word)

Nothing observed between 0.13 and 0.51. The threshold did not need fitting.

A WORKED EXAMPLE -- the system was right and we were wrong
  78:9 نَوْمَكُمْ scored 0.000 across three separate takes. We suspected a
  model defect: Husary scores 0.982 on the same word and text. Reciting it
  more deliberately moved it to 0.13, then to 0.99. The model had been
  correct every time; the articulation was weak. That is the behaviour a
  teacher's ear provides, and it is what the device exists to supply between
  lessons.

DEPLOYMENT  (measured on laptop CPU; Pi 4 estimated at 4-5x)
  model            whisper-base-quran, 72.6M params
  memory           ~300 MB, against 4 GB available
  per attempt      0.48s CPU  (RTF 0.08)  ->  ~2 - 2.5s on a Pi
  startup          13.6s once; load at boot, the learner never sees it
  network          none, ever

  Forced decoding is a SINGLE forward pass -- the tokens are already known,
  so there is no autoregressive generation and no beam search. That is why
  scoring is cheaper than transcription, and why base fits on a Pi without
  quantisation.

  So base can be kept. tiny is 2x faster but mis-heard نَجْعَلِ as نَجْعَلْ
  and would have shown a learner an error they did not make.


===============================================================================
2. THE LEARNER'S USE CASES
===============================================================================

Enumerated first, because if the ML does not fall out of these it is
decoration.

SESSION
   1. Choose a verse
   2. Hear it recited correctly, whole
   3. Hear it slowly or phrase by phrase
   4. Recite it back without looking at a screen
   5. Learn whether that was acceptable
   6. Learn WHERE it was not
   7. Retry with the correction in mind
   8. Be told when to stop and ask a teacher
   9. Move on, or repeat
  10. Return tomorrow and not start from zero

NOT YET HANDLED
  11. Recite from memory with no reference played first
  12. Recite several verses in a row
        -> now nearly free: forced decoding handles multi-verse text as
           easily as single. This is how learners actually practise.
  13. Stop mid-verse, think, continue -- hesitation is not an error
  14. A different but legitimate tajweed convention than the reference
  15. A noisy room with other children reciting nearby
  16. Shared use by siblings of different ages

TEACHER
  17. Which verses this student struggles with
  18. Whether the struggle is improving
  19. Trust that the device is not teaching something wrong


===============================================================================
3. THE APPROACHES, AND WHY THREE WERE NOT ENOUGH
===============================================================================

v1  CLASSIFIER SHOPPING ON MFCC / DTW FEATURES
    82 AUC, F1 ~60. Swapping MLP, logistic regression and tree models over
    the same features moved it a couple of points each time. Three model
    families with very different inductive biases all capping out is the
    signature of a REPRESENTATION limit, not a model limit.

v3  DURATION DEVIATION   (shipped; still the fallback)
    detection AUPRC 0.901 (baseline 0.700), holdout 0.915, artifact control
    gap -0.007, top-1 localization 0.543.
      truncation 0.944   word_deletion 0.758   repetition 0.344
      madd 0.380-0.388   substitution 0.360    near-miss 0.250
    Best single feature, mean_log_dev, reached 0.918 holdout AP alone
    against 0.925 for logistic regression over seven features. Seven
    thousandths was not worth a training dependency, so v3 ships the single
    feature. Strong on STRUCTURE, blind on SEGMENTAL content -- duration
    cannot see a wrong phoneme of the right length.

v4a CONTRASTIVE ACOUSTIC WORD EMBEDDINGS   (implemented, abandoned)
    val AP 0.54 (unseen words, seen voices) but holdout AP 0.064 (unseen
    voices). It learned WORDS and not SPEAKER INVARIANCE.

    THE REASON, and it is the useful finding: speaker-invariance work in the
    literature trains on hundreds to thousands of speakers. This corpus has
    SIX. With six voices there are not enough directions of speaker
    variation to separate "voice" from "word", so the model encodes voice --
    because with six voices, voice IS a reliable cue. No architecture or
    loss change fixes that; it is a data limit.

    Frozen pretrained features were tried next and did improve the raw
    representation. Word AP on held-out voices, against ~0.0017 chance:
      DistilHuBERT        0.011   (2 layers; curve still rising at the top)
      whisper-base        0.108
      whisper-base-quran  0.143   (same size as whisper-base: the delta is
                                   Qur'anic fine-tuning)
    But the trained head still produced z scores straddling per-word
    thresholds ranging 5.6 to 23.4, with correct recitation and real errors
    overlapping. The literature names this exact failure: a decision
    threshold fitted this way "can be error specific and thus very hard to
    generalize".

    Also worth recording: an UNTRAINED head scored 0.993 on the
    device-localization metric we had built, because that metric asked which
    of ~3 words in an ayah this is -- easy, and not what the device needs.
    Two evaluation designs in a row measured something adjacent to the real
    task.

v4b FORCED DECODING   (shipped)
    Section 0. The model was already trained to do this; we were not asking
    it the question it could answer.

WHAT THE ABANDONED PIPELINE COST
  pairs.py, features.py, head.py, embscore.py, embeddings.npz,
  word_thresholds.json, feats_tiny/ (891 MB), cache/ (1.4 GB).
  The code is kept for the write-up; the artefacts are deleted.


===============================================================================
4. WHY THE MODEL CHOICE IS DOING THE WORK
===============================================================================

whisper-base scored 0.108 word AP where whisper-base-quran scored 0.143 --
same architecture, same parameter count; the difference is Qur'anic
fine-tuning. In the final system that same fine-tuning is the difference
between 0.99 and 0.000 separation.

Tarteel's models are trained on crowdsourced recitations from thousands of
ordinary users -- learners and children, not eight professional reciters.
That is exactly the speaker diversity this corpus lacks, already in the
weights. Without that model there is no result here, and the write-up should
say so plainly.


===============================================================================
5. THE REFERENCE TEXT   (three rules, all derived empirically)
===============================================================================

Forced decoding scores audio against TEXT. If the orthography differs from
what the model saw in training, probabilities drop for reasons that have
nothing to do with pronunciation, and the device flags correct recitation.

Scoring a professional reciter -- correct by construction -- against every
Tanzil variant, mean worst-token over 40 ayahs:

  quran-simple-plain   0.87        <- use this
  quran-simple         0.65
  quran-uthmani        0.25
  quran-simple-min     0.00
  quran-uthmani-min    0.00
  quran-simple-clean   0.00

RULE 1  use quran-simple-plain. The Tarteel models were fine-tuned on
        imlaei script; Uthmani orthography penalises correct recitation
        heavily.

RULE 2  strip U+0670 ARABIC LETTER SUPERSCRIPT ALEF. This ONE codepoint
        accounted for 9 of 11 mismatched ayahs -- every alif-maqsura ending
        (مُوسَىٰ, إِلَىٰ, طَغَىٰ) and الرَّحْمَٰن. Removing it took the
        reciter from 11/60 failures to 2/60.

RULE 3  strip the basmala from ayah 1 of every surah except 1 and 9. Tanzil
        prepends it; the audio does not contain it, so the model is asked to
        find words that were never recited and the verse collapses.
        Take the prefix FROM THE TEXT -- verse 1:1 IS the basmala. Two
        hand-typed attempts failed because the superscript alef in
        الرَّحْمَٰن does not survive a shell paste reliably.

  After all three: mean worst-token 0.9994, 0 of 60 ayahs below 0.5.

DIACRITICS ARE REQUIRED. simple-clean and every harakat-stripped variant
scored exactly 0.000. The model genuinely hears vowelling, which is what
makes the per-token diagnosis (kasra vs sukun) meaningful rather than
inferred from context.


===============================================================================
6. IMPLEMENTATION DETAILS THAT DECIDED WHETHER IT WORKED
===============================================================================

None of these are in any paper. Each was the difference between working and
not working.

  THE FORCED TASK PREFIX. Whisper's decoder always begins with
  <|startoftranscript|><|ar|><|transcribe|><|notimestamps|>. Omitting it
  makes the FIRST real token collapse for everyone -- a professional reciter
  scored logP -15 on a word he recites perfectly. Score the body tokens
  only; the prefix positions are forced and carry no signal.

  WORST TOKEN, NOT MEAN. One wrong diacritic in an eight-token word barely
  moves the mean but IS the error. Measured: سُبَاتًا correct 0.994 worst,
  deliberate ص 0.000 worst.

  SILENCE TRIMMING. DTW must map every frame onto the reference, so a pause
  before the learner starts is absorbed into the FIRST word's span. Observed
  live: a 0.6s lead-in took a word from z=4 to z=16 under the embedding
  scorer.

  CLIPPED ONSETS. A learner who starts reciting before the recorder catches
  up loses the first consonant, and the device flags a word they said
  perfectly. Confirmed as the cause of one false flag.

  A RANDOM MODEL MUST FAIL YOUR METRIC. An untrained head scored 0.993 on
  our device-localization metric. Test this before trusting any number.

  ROWS MUST DIVIDE EVENLY. Eight reciters saying identical text cannot
  produce a fractional row count. That single check caught two separate
  parse bugs in the corpus work -- an inclusive/exclusive index error and
  missing word indices -- both of which passed every other structural test.


===============================================================================
7. WHAT THE DEVICE SAYS
===============================================================================

Unchanged from v3, and still justified by the error rates rather than by
preference.

  below threshold        silence. Ten verses in a row produced no
                         interruption; that is what makes a replay mean
                         something when it happens.
  one word flagged       play the learner's own rendition of that word, then
                         the reference, twice. No verdict is spoken. The
                         replay IS the message.
  every word flagged     "that sounds like a different verse" -- forced
                         decoding detects this decisively, against all 6236
                         verses rather than the +/-3 neighbour window v3
                         used.
  after three attempts   "practice this one with your teacher", and move on.

The model now also names the specific weak TOKEN -- the kasra on نَجْعَلِ,
the sin in سُبَاتًا. This is NOT surfaced to the learner by default: "you
said sin as sad" is a strong claim to make to a child, and the replay is
sufficient. It is the right content for a teacher's review log.


===============================================================================
8. WHAT IS NOT YET TRUE
===============================================================================

  ONE SPEAKER. Every uncontaminated result rests on one adult voice. One
  speaker is an anecdote. The reference reciters are likely inside the
  model's fine-tuning data (Tarteel trained on EveryAyah), so held-out
  reciter numbers are optimistic and should be read as an upper bound.

  NO REAL LEARNER ERRORS. The synthetic set passed its artifact control
  three times (final gap -0.007), which proves the system is not detecting
  splice seams. It cannot prove the error taxonomy matches what students
  actually do. QDAT (1500+ voices, correct and incorrect recitation of three
  tajweed rules) is public and would help.

  NO TEACHER HAS SEEN IT. Nobody has asked a Kaduna teacher what students
  get wrong most, or whether the flags are pedagogically right. One
  twenty-minute conversation could redirect the error taxonomy and would be
  worth more than several points of any metric.

  PI NUMBERS ARE ESTIMATED, at 4-5x the measured laptop CPU time. Not
  measured on the hardware.

  everyayah AUDIO REDISTRIBUTION and the Tanzil TEXT LICENCE are both still
  open in ATTRIBUTION.txt. The text is now actually needed, so rule 1 makes
  the Tanzil question live. Resolve before any field deployment, not after.
